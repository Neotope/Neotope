How to Play

Objective: Be the first player to connect 4 of your colored pieces in a line—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally!

How to Drop: Tap the large column buttons above the board (or tap any cell in a column) to drop your piece into that column.

Undo Feature: Made a mistake? Press the "Undo Move" button anytime during practice to step back and try another move.

Customization: Visit Settings to adjust AI difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), switch to Pass & Play with visiting friends, or try smaller/larger grids!
